NodNod Support

It didn’t ring. NodNod rings from your iPhone’s location, so it needs Location and Alarms allowed for it in Settings; the app says so when either is off. Underground, in a long tunnel or with the battery low, the location can come late or not at all: for a stop that matters, set the backup alarm too.

Does it ring on silent? Yes. It rings through Apple’s alarms, like the Clock app’s, so it sounds on silent, in a Focus and on the Lock Screen.

Do I need a connection? Searching for a stop and drawing the map need one. Once a trip has started, the alarm rings from your iPhone’s location.

Restore a purchase. In NodNod, open NodNod Pro and tap Restore Purchase. Purchases belong to your Apple Account, so they come back on a new iPhone or after reinstalling.

Refunds. Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds: request one from Apple.

Your data. What you save stays on your iPhone; what NodNod sends to Apple Maps is in its privacy policy. There is nothing to sign in to, and we cannot see or recover what you save. Deleting NodNod deletes its data from the iPhone.
