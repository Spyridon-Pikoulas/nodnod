As many as you like, each to one stop, with no account.

  • An alarm before your stop, even on silent
  • Your stop picked on the map, and the trip shown on it

NodNod Pro, $3.99 once

One purchase, no subscription. The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy.

  • Saved and recent stops. Your stops on Home, one tap to a trip.
  • Routines. Your usual trip starts on its own on its days, once you set off.
  • Routes with changes. Wakes you at the change, then at your stop.
  • One-press start. From Siri, a widget, Control Center or the Action button.
  • Over 20 alarm sounds, from gentle to firm.

A backup, not a guarantee

NodNod rings from your iPhone’s location, which can come late or not at all underground, in tunnels or with the battery low. For a stop that matters, set the backup alarm too.

Yours alone

No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your stops and routines are kept on your iPhone, and your last position only until the trip ends. Search and the map send your position to Apple Maps. The privacy policy says what is kept and where.
